Predicting Which Customers Will Leave

Customer churn prediction using a decision tree

The Business Question

Which SignalBay Wireless customers are likely to cancel?

The Data

2,400 practice customers with their contract, monthly charge and support calls.

What I Did

I trained a decision tree in Python to sort customers into "stay" and "leave".

What I Found

Customers on month-to-month contracts who call support often are the most likely to leave.

Why It Matters

The company can call these customers first and fix their problems before they cancel.

Tools

Python pandas scikit-learn
