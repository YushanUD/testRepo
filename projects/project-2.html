Can a Team of Trees Beat One Tree?

The Business Question

Can we predict customer churn more accurately than with a single decision tree?

What I Did

I trained a random forest: hundreds of decision trees that each vote, and the majority wins.

What I Found

The forest was more accurate on new customers than one tree, because the trees' mistakes cancel out.

Why It Matters

Better predictions mean the company calls the right customers and wastes less money.

Tools

Python scikit-learn RandomForestClassifier
