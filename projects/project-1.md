# Predicting Which Customers Will Leave

**The business question:** Which SignalBay Wireless customers are likely to cancel?

**The data:** 2,400 practice customers with their contract, monthly charge and support calls.

**What I did:** I trained a decision tree in Python to sort customers into "stay" and "leave".

**What I found:** Customers on month-to-month contracts who call support often are the most likely to leave.

**Why it matters:** The company can call these customers first and fix their problems before they cancel.

**Tools:** Python, pandas, scikit-learn
